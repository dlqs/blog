You should join a book club

November 25, 2026

Like most people these days, I too, carry around a little attention sucking brick in my pocket. As a result my attention span has almost certainly been permanently damaged. I admit, I’ve been fully Instagram Reels pilled - it’s a good product! - much to the chagrin of my former book loving self.

Which is kind of ironic, since I also happen to run a book club. You’d think that I’d be a reading enthusiast already. But the truth is, I’m not - I’m just like the rest of you.

I do want to be though! And I happen to like organizing things. So what I found is that, running a book club itself can help me read, by providing external motivation to do so. I’m the kind of person who needs someone to push me to do things, so this happens to work out well for me.

For most of us, we get our external motivations first from our parents, then school, then work. But outside of these venues, we… don’t really have anything to drive us in a particular direction.

(I mean, another way to look at this is as freedom/“free time”, but most people really spend it doomscrolling anyways. Is that really freedom?)

The point is, external motivations work. So join a book club today, even if you don’t currently read. All it requires is that you want to. Don’t doomscroll your dreams away.
