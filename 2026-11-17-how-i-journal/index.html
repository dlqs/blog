How I journal

November 17, 2026

So I’ve been journaling for a while now and I figured it’d be helpful to write down how I do it, in case this helps anyone else.

For context, I’ve been doing this on and off for the last five years. While I try to write every day, realistically, it’s closer to every few days. Sometimes I miss entire weeks. But that’s fine: this is your personal journal, you can write it however you like, whenever you like, as long as you write in the end, it’ll be beneficial. Any amount of writing is better than no writing.

In terms of the actual medium that I write on, I have the following hard requirements

  • Private: this rules out physical journals, unless I also get a physical safe and lock it up, but that seems too tedious
  • Searchable: valuable to be able to Ctrl-F a particular keyword
  • Simple: no distracting nonsense

You probably have heard of the note taking app Notion, which is what I started with, which did the job well. However since 2023 their product has’t really improved and in fact degraded, specifically, enshittified. For example they never bothered implementing password protected notes, a top requested feature on their own subreddit. The last straw for me was their attempt to shove AI down their users’ throats (Press Space for AI) which made the app annoyingly unusable. Maybe this is surprising for the folks at Notion, but I want to press Space for Space, not for AI, especially when it comes to my own freakin’ journal. Either way, Notion isn’t even a note taking app anymore, and I’ve since moved off it.

My current workflow is just to jot everything down in one large markdown file which I read from and write to with Obsidian. This is the simplest setup I can think of and it works remarkably well. I simply keep prepending entries to the top. Obsidian has a ton of plugins, of these I use the encryption plugins and Obsidian Sync (a paid product), and nothing else. You’d think that the file size would be a problem, but so far I’m at 50,000 words and 375Kb. For most computers purchased in the last ten years this is not a problem.
